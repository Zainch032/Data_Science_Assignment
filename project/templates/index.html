🔑 Key Findings from EDA

  • Contract Type: Month-to-month contracts account for 88.5% of all customer churn.
  • High Risk Segment: Customers with Month-to-month + No Tech Support + Tenure < 12 months churn at 65.57%.
  • Payment Method: Electronic check users churn at 45.3%, compared to ~15% for automatic credit card/bank transfer.
  • Service Depth: Customers with 4+ add-ons churn at < 8% vs 26% for customers with 0 add-ons.

⚡ Model Evaluation & Justification

  • Primary Metric: Optimized for Recall (82.4%) because missing a churner costs NexaTel ~$74/month in lifetime value.
  • Model Architecture: LightGBM Classifier tuned with SMOTE oversampling on training data to handle class imbalance.
  • Scaler: StandardScaler fit on training numeric features only to prevent data leakage.
